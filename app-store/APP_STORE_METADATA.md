## Description
Work + Gym Coach helps people build a realistic strength and body-composition plan around the way they actually work.

Create a standard or rotating work cycle, add a secondary or variable monthly schedule, and let the planner surface practical training opportunities without treating high-load workdays like ordinary recovery days.

Training includes a four-workout upper/lower rotation, equipment-aware exercise alternatives, set/repetition/RIR logging, completed-session history, estimated strength trends, progression coaching and fatigue-aware volume guidance.

Nutrition tools include calorie and macro targets, food logging, saved foods and recipes, packaged-food search, barcode lookup, water tracking and adherence trends.

Body Stats tracks weight, BMI, body-fat estimates, waist and other measurements, lean and fat mass, FFMI, estimated BMR, trends and goal progress. Recovery inputs can include sleep, steps and resting heart rate.

Your core records are stored on your device by default. No account is required. You can export backups and optionally configure encrypted private sync to your own compatible server.

Work + Gym Coach is a fitness planning and educational tool and is not a medical device or substitute for professional medical advice.

## Suggested App Review notes
The app does not require login or account creation. Most user-entered schedule, training, nutrition, body and recovery records remain in device-local storage. Open Food Facts is contacted only for packaged-food search/barcode lookup. Optional WebDAV sync is user-configured and transmits an encrypted backup to the user's chosen server. Camera access is requested only after the user explicitly starts barcode or schedule-image scanning. Manual alternatives are available when camera access is denied.
