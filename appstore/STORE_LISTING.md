Plan training around real work shifts, recovery and nutrition — then let the coach adapt as your schedule and performance change.

## Description
Work + Gym Coach is built for people whose training has to fit around real life.

Set up your own work pattern — including standard weeks, rotating shifts, variable monthly schedules or no work schedule at all — and the app helps you decide when to train, what workout comes next and how hard to push.

TRAIN AROUND YOUR SCHEDULE
• Build repeating 7–28 day work cycles
• Add a second variable or monthly work schedule
• See double-job, single-job, off and unknown days clearly
• Get planned workouts on your best recovery days
• Optionally make every single-job day available for training

ADAPTIVE WORKOUT COACHING
• Four rotating hypertrophy-focused workouts
• Exercise alternatives when equipment is unavailable
• Track weight, reps and RIR for every working set
• Estimated 1RM and volume trends
• Exercise-specific progression recommendations
• Fatigue and deload guidance based on performance and recovery
• Permanent completed-workout history

NUTRITION & BODY COMPOSITION
• Daily calorie and macro tracking
• Food search, barcode lookup, saved foods and recipes
• Weight, waist and body-fat tracking
• BMI, lean mass, fat mass, FFMI and estimated BMR
• Weight-trend and calorie-adherence coaching

RECOVERY
• Track sleep, steps and resting heart rate
• Recovery-readiness scoring
• Workload-aware training recommendations

YOUR DATA
• No account required for core use
• Local-first storage
• Export/import backups
• Optional encrypted private sync to a server you control
• Delete-all-data control inside the app

Work + Gym Coach provides educational fitness and nutrition estimates and is not medical advice.
