Privacy Policy

Work + Workout · Effective August 23, 2026

Local-first with optional account sync

Work + Workout stores planner information on your device while you use the app. When account services are enabled and you choose to migrate or sync a device, eligible planner data is also stored in your Work + Workout account so it can be restored on your other devices.

Account information

If you create an account, the authentication provider processes information such as your email address, password credentials in protected form, authentication/session information, and optional display name. Work + Workout uses Supabase for account authentication and cloud database services in the current architecture.

Planner and fitness information

The app may process information you choose to enter or import, including your name, height, age/calorie-estimation setting, goals, work schedules, shift times, commute times, recurring commitments, sleep target, training preferences, equipment access, workout history, weights/repetitions/RIR, food preferences, nutrition entries, weight, waist and other body measurements, and recovery information. When you use account migration or sync, eligible locally stored planner records can be uploaded to your account. Old WebDAV passwords, passphrases and private sync credentials are intentionally excluded from account migration.

AI Coach and onboarding

When you use AI Coach or request AI-assisted onboarding, relevant information from your profile, plan, schedule, recent training or nutrition context and the text you submit may be sent through the Work + Workout server to OpenAI to generate a response. AI chat text may be stored with your account so the service can support continuity and troubleshooting. AI-generated guidance is not medical care.

Equipment photos

If you explicitly attach or take a gym-equipment photo for AI Coach, a resized copy is transmitted through the Work + Workout server to the AI provider so the equipment can be analyzed. Work + Workout does not intentionally write the image itself into its chat-message database; the database records that an equipment photo was used and stores the resulting text conversation. The AI provider may process request data under its applicable service and privacy terms.

Camera and schedule photos

Camera access is also used when you choose barcode scanning or schedule-photo scanning. Schedule OCR is designed to run in the app/browser and the schedule image is not intentionally uploaded to the Work + Workout account. Barcode numbers and food search terms may be sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve product information.

Food preferences and familiar foods

Onboarding may ask which foods and cuisines you already eat, along with restrictions, cooking preferences and budget preference. These are used to generate nutrition suggestions that are more familiar and practical for you. Packaged-food search and barcode lookup may use Open Food Facts, which is governed by its own privacy practices.

Health and recovery files

If you import an Apple Health export or CSV file, the app processes the file to extract supported values such as weight, sleep, steps and resting heart rate. The original imported file is not intentionally uploaded through the normal import flow. Extracted values stored in the planner may become part of account sync if you explicitly migrate or sync your app data.

Backups and private sync

Manual backup files can be exported by you. If you separately configure a WebDAV/Nextcloud-style private sync server, encrypted backup data is sent to the server address you provide. Those WebDAV credentials remain device-local and are excluded from Work + Workout account migration.

Service providers

The current architecture may use Supabase for authentication/database services, OpenAI for AI-generated responses and image understanding, Open Food Facts for packaged-food/barcode information, Vercel or another server hosting provider for secured application APIs, and infrastructure providers required to deliver the app. These providers process information only as necessary for the features you choose to use and under their applicable terms.

Analytics, advertising and sale of data

The current product design does not include behavioral advertising or developer-operated cross-app tracking, and the developer does not sell personal data. If analytics or advertising practices change, this policy and the applicable App Store privacy disclosures must be updated before those changes are released.

Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on a device until you delete it, clear app/site data, or uninstall/reset the app where applicable. Signed-in cloud data remains associated with the account until it is deleted under the product's retention practices. The app includes a permanent account-deletion action; deleting the cloud account does not automatically erase backup files you previously exported to your own storage.

Security

Account access uses authenticated sessions and database row-level access controls. Server secrets are not embedded in the client application. The app also offers encrypted manual backups. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure, so you should protect your devices and exported files.

Children

Work + Workout is not designed for children under 13. Commercial distribution may apply a higher minimum age depending on region and product positioning.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the product changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, where required, by updating in-app or store disclosures.

Contact and support

For support or privacy questions, use the Work + Workout support page.
