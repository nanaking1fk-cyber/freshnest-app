Privacy Policy

Work + Gym Coach · Effective August 23, 2026

Privacy-first design

Work + Gym Coach is designed to function without creating an account. Your profile, work schedules, workouts, food diary, body measurements and recovery entries are stored locally on your device by default.

Information you enter

The app may store information you choose to enter, including your name, work schedule, shift times, sleep target, training logs, weights/repetitions/RIR, nutrition entries, weight, waist and other body measurements, and recovery information.

Camera and photos

Camera access is used only when you choose barcode scanning or schedule-photo scanning. Schedule photos are processed on-device/in the app and are not intentionally uploaded to the developer. Barcode numbers may be sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve product information.

Food search

When you search packaged foods or look up a barcode, the search text or barcode may be sent to Open Food Facts. Their service is governed by their own privacy practices. Generic built-in food entries do not require a third-party lookup.

Health and recovery files

If you import an Apple Health export or CSV file, the app processes that file locally to extract supported values such as weight, sleep, steps and resting heart rate. The developer does not receive the imported file through the normal local-use workflow.

Backups and sync

Manual backup files can be exported by you. If you configure optional private WebDAV/Nextcloud-style sync, encrypted backup data is sent to the server address you provide. The developer does not provide or control that server.

Analytics, advertising and sale of data

The current version does not include behavioral advertising or developer-operated analytics, and the developer does not sell your personal data.

Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app/site data, uninstall the app where applicable, or use the in-app Delete All Data function. Backup copies you created remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Security

The app offers encrypted backup export for sensitive local data. No method of storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, so you should protect your device and any exported backup files.

Children

Work + Gym Coach is not designed for children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the product changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.

Contact and support

For support or privacy questions, use the Work + Gym Coach support page.
