Help & privacy

Work + Workout Support

Help with accounts, schedules, training history, nutrition, barcode scanning, calendar sync, backups and body statistics.

Run the system check

Open More → Legal & privacy → System Check. It checks the main tabs, local storage, encryption support and key coaching functions.

Protect your data first

Open More → Account & data → Data & backup and download an encrypted backup if the app is responsive. Keep its passphrase separately. Export work hours and pay from Calendar as well.

Signing in or resetting your password

Confirmation opened Sign in: sign in with the email and password you used to create your account. This can happen when you open the email in another browser or device.

Reset link expired or will not open: request a fresh link from Sign in → Forgot password, then open the newest email in the same browser or app that requested it. Keep that browser open. If you see a wait message, let the timer finish before requesting another email.

Your planner looks empty: check that you signed in with the original account email. Stay online while restore finishes. Do not create another account, clear browser data or delete the app to troubleshoot missing records. Contact support if restore still fails.

Photo scans and privacy choices

Your saved privacy choices are remembered. If you previously turned off cloud sync or AI photo analysis, review them under More → Account & data. You can change your choices without resetting your planner. Your phone may separately ask for camera or photo access.

For Meal Scan, choose a clear photo, tap Analyze, check each food and serving size, then save. A scan is an estimate, not a nutrition label. For work rosters, use a sharp, straight photo, select your row, and review dates and shift times before saving.

Hours, pay and sharing

Confirm the hours you actually worked and check breaks, overtime rules and your pay rate. Pay totals are estimates, not a payslip or tax calculation. When sharing a calendar PDF, review the dates and included items; workouts and notes are optional.

Common fixes

Old version showing: fully close the Home Screen app, reopen while online, or open the latest browser version once.

Camera scanner unavailable: confirm camera permission and try the photo-barcode option or manual barcode entry.

Schedule OCR inaccurate: rotate/crop the photo and review every detected date before saving. Unknown dates remain unknown rather than becoming days off.

Workout history missing: open Training and use History. Only sessions saved with Complete Workout become permanent history.

Privacy or health-data request

Use private email—not the public issue tracker. To access, correct, delete or ask about personal or consumer health data, email info@bibiniifarms.com with the subject “Work + Workout Privacy Request.” Include the account email if applicable, but do not send your password, full health export or unnecessary medical details.

Get help or report a problem

Email us privately with the app version, your device, what you tapped and what happened. Do not send passwords, confirmation links, payment details or health records. Cover personal information in any screenshot.

Email support

For a general technical bug with no personal information, you can also use the public issue tracker. Anything posted there is public.
